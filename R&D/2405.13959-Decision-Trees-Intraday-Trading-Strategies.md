# Decision Trees for Intuitive Intraday Trading Strategies

**Authors:** Sharath Chandra Nirmala, Tiruveedi Poornoday  
**Affiliation:** Department of CSE, The National Institute of Engineering, Mysuru, India  
**arXiv:** [2405.13959](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13959)  
**Date:** May 2024  

## Abstract
This paper investigates the use of decision trees to construct intraday trading strategies based on technical indicators for individual equities in the NIFTY50 index. By letting decision trees learn rule combinations from indicators such as RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, and moving averages, the approach generates stock‑specific trading rules without manual rule design. Backtesting shows that decision‑tree‑based strategies outperform a simple buy‑and‑hold baseline for many stocks, offering a systematic, data‑driven alternative to traditional indicator‑based trading.

## Summary
The authors treat technical indicators as features and train classification/regression decision trees to predict short‑term price direction or returns. The resulting tree encodes intuitive if‑then rules (e.g., “IF RSI < 30 AND MACD > 0 THEN BUY”). Because the model is learned per stock, it adapts to each security’s characteristics. Empirical results indicate improved performance over buy‑and‑hold, suggesting decision trees can automate rule discovery for intraday trading.
