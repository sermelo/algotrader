# Intraday Power Trading for Imbalance Markets: An Adaptive Risk-Averse Strategy using Mixture Models

**Authors:** Robin Bruneel, Mathijs Schuurmans, Panagiotis Patrinos  
**arXiv:** [2402.01215v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01215v2)  
**Date:** 21 Jan 2025 (v2)  

## Abstract
This paper introduces a novel intraday power trading strategy for single‑price balancing markets. The strategy uses a mixture model to forecast system imbalance prices and formulates a stochastic optimization problem with decision‑dependent distributions. It addresses (i) the impact of trading positions on the forecasted price and (ii) model uncertainty by adding a coherent risk measure. The risk‑measure tuning parameter is adapted online based on recent performance. Validation on the Belgian electricity market shows higher absolute profits and fewer trades.

## Summary
The proposed method combines probabilistic price forecasting (via mixture models) with risk‑averse stochastic optimization. By modeling how trades affect the imbalance price and penalizing uncertainty, the strategy adapts its risk tolerance in real time. Empirical results confirm improved profit‑to‑trade ratio, making it suitable for markets where margin is small and model risk is significant.
