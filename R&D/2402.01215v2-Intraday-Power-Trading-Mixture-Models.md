# Intraday Power Trading for Imbalance Markets: An Adaptive Risk-Averse Strategy using Mixture Models

**Authors:** Robin Bruneel, Mathijs Schuurmans, Panagiotis Patrinos  
**Affiliation:** KU Leuven, Department of Electrical Engineering (ESAT-STADIUS), Belgium  
**arXiv:** [2402.01215v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01215v2) (a v3 from 15 Apr 2025 also exists; the v2 text is titled "...for Balancing Markets..." and targets Applied Energy)  
**Date:** v1 2 Feb 2024, v2 21 Jan 2025  
**Full text:** [papers/2402.01215v2-Intraday-Power-Trading-Mixture-Models.md](papers/2402.01215v2-Intraday-Power-Trading-Mixture-Models.md)

## Abstract (paraphrased)
An intraday power trading strategy for single-price balancing markets. A mixture model forecasts
system imbalance prices, and trading is posed as a stochastic optimisation with decision-dependent
distributions, accounting for how the trader's own positions move the imbalance price. A coherent
risk measure handles model uncertainty, and its tuning parameter is adapted online from recent
performance. Simulated on the Belgian market with real-time data, the adaptive strategy earns
higher absolute profits with fewer trades.

## Summary
Probabilistic price forecasting combined with risk-averse optimisation. Quarter-hourly positions are
taken on the Belgian intraday market about one hour five minutes before delivery. The adaptive
risk parameter trades less and earns more per trade than fixed-risk versions.

## Caveats
- Specific to electricity balancing markets; the mechanism (imbalance settlement) has no equity analogue.

## Relevance to algotrader
Very low. The general idea of adapting risk aversion to recent performance could transfer, but
nothing here is directly testable on our data.
