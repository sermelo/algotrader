# Intraday trading strategy based on time series and machine learning for Chinese stock market

**Authors:** Qinan Wang, Yaomu Zhou, Junhao Shen  
**Affiliation:** Lyle School of Engineering, Southern Methodist University  
**arXiv:** [2103.13507v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.13507v1)  
**Date:** 24 Mar 2021  
**Full text:** [papers/2103.13507v1-Intraday-Trading-Strategy-Time-Series-ML-Chinese-Stock.md](papers/2103.13507v1-Intraday-Trading-Strategy-Time-Series-ML-Chinese-Stock.md)

## Abstract (paraphrased)
An intraday strategy for Chinese A-shares under the T+1 settlement rule, combining Markowitz
portfolio optimisation with a multilayer perceptron (MLP) that predicts intraday prices, using
Shenzhen and Shanghai exchange data. The authors present the combination as feasible. The abstract
gives no return figure.

## Method
- **Portfolio:** Markowitz maximum-Sharpe portfolio of 30 stocks, from daily closes January to
  September 2020.
- **Intraday trading:** because of T+1, the strategy holds the portfolio and trades around it.
  An MLP trained on 1-minute closes from 09:30 to 11:20 predicts the 14:50 price; extra shares are
  bought before the midday close and sold at 14:50. Retrained daily. No trade if the predicted move
  is under 0.1% (fees assumed 0.05%).
- **Data:** 1-minute bars for **October 2020 only**, from Xueqiu Finance.

## Results
- Intraday trading gained **1.41% in October 2020**, which the paper annualises to **18.24% a year**.
- Separately, the buy-and-hold portfolio gained 8.02% in October (annualised by the paper to 152.35%).

## Caveats
- The "18.24% yearly return" is one month of results annualised, not a measured yearly return.
- One month of test data, and the model is fitted and evaluated within that same month.
- T+1 mechanics are specific to China and do not apply to US equities.

## Relevance to algotrader
Low. Too little evidence to justify implementing.
