# Intraday trading strategy based on time series and machine learning for Chinese stock market

**Authors:** Qinan Wang, Yaomu Zhou, Junhao Shen  
**Affiliation:** Lyle School of Engineering, Southern Methodist University  
**arXiv:** [2103.13507v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.13507v1)  
**Date:** 24 Mar 2021  

## Abstract
This article proposes an intraday trading strategy under the T+1 rule using Markowitz portfolio optimization and a Multilayer Perceptron (MLP) for stock price prediction, utilizing data from the Shenzhen and Shanghai Stock Exchanges. The Markowitz optimization identifies profitable portfolios, while the MLP predicts intraday price movements. Combining both yields a strategy that achieved a yearly return of 18.24% in the Chinese stock market, demonstrating the feasibility of integrating optimization and machine learning for intraday trading.

## Summary
The paper combines mean‑variance optimization (Markowitz) with a neural network (MLP) to generate intraday trading signals under China’s T+1 settlement constraint. The approach first selects a portfolio of stocks via Markowitz, then uses MLP to forecast short‑term price movements, triggering trades when predictions exceed a threshold. Empirical results show consistent profitability, highlighting the potential of hybrid quantitative‑ML methods for intraday strategies.
