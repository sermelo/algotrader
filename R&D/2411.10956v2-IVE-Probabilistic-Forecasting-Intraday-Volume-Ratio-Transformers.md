# IVE: Enhanced Probabilistic Forecasting of Intraday Volume Ratio with Transformers

**Authors:** Hanwool Lee, Heehwan Park  
**Affiliation:** Shinhan Securities, NCSOFT (South Korea)  
**arXiv:** [2411.10956v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10956v2)  
**Date:** v1 17 Nov 2024, v2 9 Mar 2025  
**Full text:** [papers/2411.10956v2-IVE-Probabilistic-Forecasting-Intraday-Volume-Ratio-Transformers.md](papers/2411.10956v2-IVE-Probabilistic-Forecasting-Intraday-Volume-Ratio-Transformers.md)

## Abstract (paraphrased)
A Transformer model (the Intraday Volume Estimator, IVE) that predicts 1-minute intraday volume
ratios for VWAP execution. The ratio is modelled after a log-normal transformation, with external
volume features, time information and stock characteristics as inputs. An encoder-decoder
Transformer with a distribution head extends point forecasts to the mean and standard deviation of
the volume ratio. Tested on high-liquidity Korean and US stocks.

## Summary
The probabilistic output is used to anticipate volume spikes and to schedule orders against a VWAP
target. A simple live trading agent ran in the Korean market for about two and a half months with
350 million KRW of capital, reporting average performance of 7.14 bp on buy orders and 2.10 bp on
sell orders (standard deviations 43.8 bp and 18.8 bp). The authors describe this as comparable to
VWAP strategies.

## Caveats
- An execution paper (how to fill orders cheaply), not a source of trading signals.
- The live result is short and noisy: the standard deviation is several times the mean.

## Relevance to algotrader
Low. Our IEX volume is a small, unrepresentative share of consolidated volume, and we do not model
execution scheduling.
