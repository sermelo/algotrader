# IVE: Enhanced Probabilistic Forecasting of Intraday Volume Ratio with Transformers

**Authors:** Hanwool Lee, Heehwan Park  
**Affiliation:** Shinhan Securities, NCSOFT (South Korea)  
**arXiv:** [2411.10956v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10956v2)  
**Date:** 09 Mar 2025  

## Abstract
This paper presents the Intraday Volume Estimator (IVE), a Transformer‑based model for probabilistic forecasting of intraday volume ratios at one‑minute granularity, aimed at improving VWAP execution. IVE extends point prediction by estimating the mean and standard deviation of volume ratios, enabling detection of volume spikes and better navigation of market volatility. Experiments on Korean and US equities show improved forecasting accuracy and utility for VWAP‑target strategies.

## Summary
IVE leverages an encoder‑decoder Transformer with a distribution head to output a full predictive distribution (mean ± std) of intraday volume ratios. This probabilistic output allows traders to anticipate extreme volume events and adjust VWAP benchmarks dynamically. The work demonstrates that modern sequence models can capture the stochastic nature of intraday volume, providing a practical tool for execution algorithms.
