# Experiment results

Generated by `python -m algotrader.experiments`; do not edit by hand. Equal-weight portfolio of all tickers, fully invested when in a position. Costs: IBKR commission + 2 bp half-spread per side (base); none (gross); IBKR + 5 bp (stress). A grid point needs a median of 5 trades per ticker in its scoring window to be chosen.

## Walk-forward (main result)

Folds start 2023-07-01, 2024-01-01, 2024-07-01, 2025-01-01, 2025-07-01, 2026-01-01; each is tuned on data before its start and tested until the next; the test periods are concatenated (3.3 years). Buy and hold of the same tickers: +76.7%, Sharpe 1.29. Alpha/beta: daily returns regressed on buy and hold. 88 configurations were tried in total; the best of that many zero-edge strategies would show a Sharpe of about 1.37 by luck alone over this length.
